A Living
Art Form
From Ghana

Before digital printing, Ghanaian cinema owners hired local artists to hand-paint massive, dramatic posters to advertise their films. These painters — working with house paint on flour sacks — created something entirely their own: bold, expressive, visceral works that reimagined Hollywood blockbusters through a Ghanaian lens.


Today these posters are collected worldwide as genuine folk art. GH Movie Posters connects you directly to this tradition — sourcing authentic hand-painted pieces and high-quality reproductions from Ghanaian artists, available as digital downloads or physical originals shipped straight to your door.
